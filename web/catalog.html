start here

Find a book with search or browse a subject below. Each title lists every copy, the rack it lives on and whether it's on the shelf. Take an available copy to the desk to borrow it: the librarian scans your card and the book's barcode.

If every copy is out, press reserve. You join a first-come, first-served queue and get a notice the moment a copy comes back. It's then held at the desk for 3 days.

You can renew a loan once from my shelf, as long as it isn't overdue and nobody is queued for it. A late return raises a fine of days late × your daily rate, and unpaid fines pause borrowing until they're settled at the desk.
